You can be confident that your data is being managed to the high standard that you need to make good decisions and drive data-led initiatives successfully.

What is Data Governance?

For a long time, data governance has been at the heart of most other data management disciplines. Without proper data governance, your MDM, PIM, analytics, integration, data quality, and data architecture efforts won't deliver their full potential. At Unifi, we offer comprehensive data governance services, starting from the advisory stages through to implementation and support. We ensure your data management initiatives are well-governed and provide maximum value.

Data governance encompasses a range of processes, including master data management (MDM) and product information management (PIM) management. Given its broad scope, there is no one-size-fits-all approach—each company has unique priorities. At Unifi, our bespoke services are tailored to meet your specific needs, offering assistance from the initial advisory and implementation stages through to ongoing support as you grow and evolve.

Why do you need data governance?

Data Governance is central to most other data management disciplines. But what benefit does it provide when working alongside other data systems?

Data quality

Data governance and data quality go hand in hand. Data quality is a combination of tooling, people, processes and how individuals interact with data.

System Implementation

Data systems are expensive, especially if the data being inputted and used is of poor quality. Strong data governance frameworks provide reassurance.

Value

Data governance combines people, process, technology and data into the simple frameworks that any Organization can adapt.

Decision Making

By implementing a data governance framework, you can make decisions based on your data systems safe in the knowledge that your tooling and data is of high quality.

Efficiency

Most challenges around data, including manual work and bad communication or siloes, are due to a lack of management of data.

Reduce Costs, Increase Revenue

The old adages of reducing costs and increasing revenue can be tied to successful data governance. When done right, your business efficiency will significantly increase.
